Rules & Commissions

Please read my rules carefully before ordering. If any point isn't respected, I may block you or refuse to work with you.

Payment is made 100% upfront via PayPal before work begins. Prices follow the current price list and may change without notice for future orders. Refunds are not possible once the sketch has started.

I'll send you the sketch of the piece; revisions before the lineart stage are free, but after lineart and colouring, changes are paid. Major changes after the finished piece is delivered are considered a new order.

A standard commission grants personal, non-commercial use (avatars, personal social media, prints for yourself). Commercial use — merch, monetised content, resale, etc. — requires the "Commercial rights" option (+100%). Copyright stays with me, including the right to post the work in my portfolio and social media, unless a private order is agreed on.

3 days to 4 weeks, depending on my workload. In the first 3 days, feel free to send all your wishes and details. If any issue comes up on my end I'll let you know about a delay. Specific deadline dates must be arranged in advance — but the piece can't be finished within the first 3 days!

I draw female and anthro characters, both SFW and NSFW.

I reserve the right to decline any order for any reason, including subject matter I'm not comfortable drawing. Clients ordering NSFW work must be 18 years or older.

If you need to cancel an order before the sketch stage, a partial refund is possible, minus the booking fee. Once lineart or colouring has begun, the order is non-refundable.
